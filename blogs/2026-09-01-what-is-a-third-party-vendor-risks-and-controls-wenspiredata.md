A third-party vendor is an external organization that provides goods, software, services, technical support, or operational capabilities to another business. From a cybersecurity perspective, a third-party vendor becomes especially important when it can access the organization’s systems, data, applications, networks, or operational technology. This is no longer only a procurement issue.
