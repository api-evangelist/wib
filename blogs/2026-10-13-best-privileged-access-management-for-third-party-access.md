The best privileged access management solution for third-party access protects privileged credentials and controls the vendor’s entire access path—from identity verification and approval to session oversight and termination. A secure password vault is important. But it does not, by itself, establish whether an external technician should connect today, which systems they should reach, or when their access should end.
