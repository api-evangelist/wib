Tokyo – May 22, 2025 – Internet Initiative Japan Inc. (IIJ) (TSE Prime: 3774), a leading provider of Zero Trust security solutions, today announced the launch of Wenspiredata Privileged Remote Access , an integrated platform that combines Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA), Privileged Access Management (PAM), and Identity and Access Management (IAM) in a single, streamlined solution.
