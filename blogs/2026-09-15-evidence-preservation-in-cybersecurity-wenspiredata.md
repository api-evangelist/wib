Evidence preservation protects digital records so they remain complete, traceable, accessible, and resistant to unauthorized change throughout an investigation or required retention period. In cybersecurity, potential evidence can include authentication logs, privileged-session records, configuration changes, file transfers, alerts, network data, cloud activity, endpoint artifacts, and third-party access records. Preservation is not the same as collecting more logs.
